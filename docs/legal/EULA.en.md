license, you agree to this Agreement.

## 1. Definitions

1. "Service" means the NEXA App, central-server, Discord bot, and related features.
2. "Provider Pool" means the system where users voluntarily contribute their local AI models for sharing.
3. "License" means the right to use the App's paid features.
4. "Trial" means using paid features free of charge for a limited period without purchasing a license.

## 2. Non-commercial Contribution vs. License Sales

1. Contribution to the Pool is **uncompensated and free of charge**. Contributors are not sellers; there is
   no payment, revenue, or settlement for contributions (ADR 0003).
2. The paid license under this Agreement is a **right to use the App's management convenience features**,
   unrelated to Pool contribution. It is not a sale of AI models or compute resources.

## 3. Free Scope

The following are provided **free forever** without a license:

1. Provider Pool participation (contributing local AI models)
2. Local AI model installation and management
3. Discord `/ask` and `/image` (image generation)
4. Viewing your own contribution status and logs

## 4. Paid Features and License

1. **Paid features**: Server Management Premium — advanced features for guild (server) admins, including
   persona customization, per-channel settings, RAG knowledge, preset sharing, and safety reports. The scope
   of paid features may change, with notice provided via release notes.
2. **Price**: USD $10 (one-time). Display currency/amount may be converted by region.
3. **Attribution**: The license is bound to one Discord account. It may be used on unlimited devices for that
   account, but **account sharing and license transfer are prohibited**.

## 5. Trial

1. From the time of service signup (first central-server account connection), you may try paid features free
   for **3 months**.
2. The trial start is your individual first account connection time, managed by the central server.
3. After the trial, paid features are disabled; the free scope in Article 3 remains available.
4. Fraudulently resetting the trial period (e.g., by creating new Discord accounts) is prohibited.

## 6. Launch Event — Lifetime Free

1. During an event period set by the Operator, users with at least one Pool contribution may apply in-app to

2. The Event Right's scope equals the $10 perpetual license in Article 4.
3. The Event Right is retained despite Operator policy changes, but "lifetime" is limited to the service
   duration in Article 12.
4. Accounts with a refund history (Article 8) cannot receive the Event Right.

1. Payment is processed through **Paddle (Merchant of Record)**, the legal entity for sale, tax, and invoicing.
2. Purchases are made in-app only, automatically linking your Discord account to the payment.

2. Upon refund, the license is **immediately revoked**, and the account cannot regain trial or event rights.

2. **Cost, Google Terms compliance, and key leakage responsibility rest with the key-owning admin.**
3. Provided cloud models/pricing may change, with notice via release notes.

2. If the Operator terminates the service, **3 months' advance notice** is given, and a **release build** that
