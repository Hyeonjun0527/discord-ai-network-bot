# NEXA Refund Policy

Version: 2.0.0 (2026-06-10)

## 1. 30-Day Unconditional Refund

You may request a full refund **for any reason within 30 days** of purchasing a NEXA license.

## 2. How to Request

1. Request via the Paddle receipt email, or contact the Operator ({{CONTACT_EMAIL}}).
2. Refunds are processed through Paddle (Merchant of Record) and returned to the original payment method.

## 3. Effect of Refund

1. Once refunded, the license is **immediately revoked** and paid features are disabled (the free scope in
   Article 3 remains available).
2. Accounts with a refund history **cannot regain trial periods or launch event rights**.
3. Repurchase is allowed.

## 4. After 30 Days

After 30 days from purchase, refunds are generally not provided, except:

1. A serious defect makes paid features unusable
2. A minor paid without legal guardian consent (cancellation requests processed as refunds)
3. Applicable law (e-commerce law, etc.) requires a refund

## 5. On Service Termination

Treatment upon service termination follows EULA Article 12.
